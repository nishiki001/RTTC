# Art bible: Programmer Art style

Everything RTTC draws must sit next to vanilla's Programmer Art (pre-1.14 textures) without standing out.

- 16×16, no HD. Animations as vertical strips like vanilla.
- Flat, noisy shading: per-pixel noise instead of smooth gradients or soft highlights.
- Chunky, high-contrast pixels; small palettes (roughly 4–8 colours per texture).
- No modern-style dark outlines around items/blocks; lighting from the top-left, lightly.
- Machines: grey/iron bodies with simple faces (IC2-classic/BuildCraft era feel), not modern bevelled panels.
- Reference: compare against vanilla programmer_art textures in the client jar, not modern textures.
